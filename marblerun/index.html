About Marble Run

Help the marble roll all the way to the finish. Solve a puzzle to clear its path, then watch it ride through 500 unique tracks. A satisfying ASMR game to relax with, one marble at a time.

5 Different Puzzles

  • Slide planks out of the way to open the barricade
  • Untangle ropes to lower the drawbridge
  • Sort the marbles by color to clear the track
  • Move bolts until every plank falls
  • Place the gears to start the lift

20 Track Pieces

  • Ferris wheels, lifts and ziplines
  • Loops, half pipes and spirals
  • Trampolines, trebuchets, dominoes and more

Sounds to Satisfy

  • Every piece has its own sound: chimes, creaks, clacks and the click of falling dominoes
  • A relaxing ASMR soundscape for every track

Stuck? Use a Hint or Undo a move whenever you need one.

Make your marble run your own with skins, trails and celebrations.

Colorblind? Turn on Color Symbols in Settings.

Play anywhere, anytime. Fully playable without internet.

Marble Run is free to play. Optional in-game purchases are available.
