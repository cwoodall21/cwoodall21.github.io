Privacy Policy

Marble Run: Puzzle Escape · Last updated October 26, 2026

Marble Run: Puzzle Escape is developed by Connor Woodall. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Marble Run has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or other contact information. The following data is collected automatically by the services the app uses:

  • Usage data: game events such as levels started and finished, puzzles solved, power-ups used, shop visits and purchases with coins, daily gifts claimed, ads offered and watched for power-ups, and how long levels take, collected by Firebase Analytics to help understand how the game is played and where it is too hard or too easy.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports and the state of the device when a crash happens, collected by Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix bugs.
  • Advertising data: your device's approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, and how you interact with ads, used by Google AdMob and its advertising partners (currently Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize) to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud. Marble Run shows ads between levels, and offers optional ads you can choose to watch for a Hint or an Unlock. In some regions you will first be asked for consent through Google's consent message. Marble Run will also ask for permission to track you across apps and websites using Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to show you ads based on your interests. If you do not, you will only see non-personalized ads, and your data will not be used to target you across apps. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Game Center data: your Game Center nickname and achievement progress, shared with Apple to show achievements.
  • Purchase information: whether you have bought Remove Ads, processed by Apple and used only to turn off the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a power-up are still offered.

Usage, crash and diagnostic data are not linked to your name or contact information. Your game progress (level, coins, power-ups, cosmetics and achievements) is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, so it can follow you to your other devices; it is not sent to us or to any third party. Reminders are scheduled on your device and sent by your device; no server is involved.

Your Rights

Marble Run has no servers of its own and holds no personal data itself. All data described in this policy is held by Google (Firebase, AdMob), Unity, Liftoff or Apple (Game Center, iCloud, StoreKit). To request access to or deletion of that data, use the tools those services provide:

  • Google data: myaccount.google.com
  • Unity data requests: unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information
  • Liftoff data requests: liftoff.ai/opt-out
  • Apple data: your Apple Account at account.apple.com
  • Ad personalization: withdraw App Tracking Transparency permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Marble Run

We do not sell personal information. California residents may contact us at the email below with privacy questions, and we will direct you to the right provider.

Children's Privacy

Marble Run is not directed to children under 13. The app does not collect personal information directly; everything collected is described above. Parents can review or turn off ad tracking at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Marble Run.
