Contact Us

For help, feedback or bug reports, email marblerunsupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 48 hours. It helps to include your device, its iOS version, and the level you were on.

Common Questions

  • My progress didn't carry over to my other device. Progress is saved in iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account, then open Marble Run on each while online.
  • I don't see Achievements in Settings. The Achievements row appears when you are signed in to Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device, then reopen Marble Run.
  • I'm not getting reminders, or I'm getting too many. Open Settings › Notifications in the game to turn each kind of reminder on or off. If they are turned off for the whole app, the game shows a button to turn them back on.
  • I bought Remove Ads but still see ads. Tap Restore Purchases in the game's Settings. If ads still show, email us with the date of your purchase.
