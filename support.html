Common Questions

  • I lost my progress. Game progress is saved to iCloud. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud on your device and that iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings.
  • I purchased Remove Ads but ads are still showing. Tap the Restore Purchases button in the shop. If the issue persists, contact us with your Apple ID and purchase date.
  • Lives are not regenerating. Lives regenerate one at a time over 30 minutes each. Make sure your device clock is set to automatic in Settings › General › Date & Time.
