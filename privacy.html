Privacy Policy

Color Pop — Last updated July 4, 2026

Color Pop is developed by Connor Woodall. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Color Pop does not collect your name, email address, or any other information that directly identifies you. The following data is collected automatically by third-party SDKs the app uses:

  • Usage data — app events such as level starts, completions, and failures, collected by Firebase Analytics to help understand how the app is used.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — crash reports and device information collected by Firebase Crashlytics to help identify and fix bugs.
  • Advertising data — device identifiers used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. Color Pop uses non-personalized ads; your data is not used for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. Color Pop does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not request permission to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Game Center data — your Game Center alias, achievement progress, and leaderboard scores, shared with Apple to power leaderboards and achievements within the app.
  • Purchase information — whether you have purchased the Remove Ads in-app purchase, processed by Apple and used only to unlock the relevant feature.

This data is not linked to your name or contact information and is processed pseudonymously. Game progress (level completion, coins, powerup inventory) is stored locally on your device and in your personal iCloud account; it is not collected by us or any third-party service.

Your Rights

Color Pop has no backend servers and holds no personal data itself. All data described in this policy is held exclusively by Google (Firebase, AdMob) or Apple (Game Center, StoreKit). To request access to or deletion of that data, use the tools provided directly by those services:

  • Google data: myaccount.google.com
  • Apple data: your Apple ID settings at appleid.apple.com

We do not sell personal information. California residents may contact us at the address below with privacy questions and we will direct you to the appropriate provider.

Children's Privacy

Color Pop does not collect personal information from any user, including children. The third-party SDKs described above collect anonymous, pseudonymous data regardless of age. If you are a parent with concerns, contact us or refer to Google's and Apple's family privacy policies linked above.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. For material changes, we will update that date; continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
