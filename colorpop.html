About Color Pop

Pop the right balloons, catch their paint, and mix the perfect color before time runs out! Color Pop presents you with a new target color each round and your goal is to match it. Each section introduces you to a new mechanic to learn and master.

A New Challenge Every Section

  • Read the wind and time your pops before balloons drift out of reach
  • Pop balloons at the right angle to guide paint around obstacles
  • Swap tapping for throwing and swipe to launch darts at moving targets
  • React fast before balloon colors disappear or run into hazards

50+ Target Colors. From Simple to Complex

  • Every round is scored on color accuracy, earning up to 3 stars.
  • Chase your personal best on every level and climb the Game Center leaderboards

Spend Your Coins to Make it Yours

  • Buy powerups to give yourself an edge
  • Unlock new bucket designs and background themes to customize your experience

Play anywhere, anytime. Fully playable without internet. Color Pop is free to play. Optional in-game purchases are available.
